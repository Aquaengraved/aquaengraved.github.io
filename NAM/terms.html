For game planning only

This player tool shows checked daily market updates for planning inside the game. It never trades for you, controls your account or promises that a price or item will still be available later.

Prices can change

Prices can change after the shown market update. The updater checks the source files and stops instead of publishing doubtful data, but mistakes or interruptions can still happen. Check important purchases in the game.

The website is not guaranteed to be available all the time and may change, pause or close. Gaps in price history stay visible; the site does not invent prices to fill them.
