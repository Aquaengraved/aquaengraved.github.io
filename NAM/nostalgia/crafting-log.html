Community crafting observations

Record your ship crafting

Record consecutive ships you actually craft. Include ordinary results as well as purple and gold ships.

View crafting chances for every port

Your file, your choice. Your entries stay in this tab until you download them. No account, identity, tracking, or automatic submission. Send the downloaded file to the project owner when you are ready.

Add a crafted ship

One record per ship. Port, ship and wood choices stay selected for the next build.

Where and what you crafted

Wood choices follow the current reviewed recipe and frame/planking catalog. Use “Unknown / not listed” when needed; add details below.

When the ship was crafted

Use the build time, not the time you send the file. “Use current UTC time” is for a ship you just crafted.

The random result

Record total slot capacity: if the card says “1/3”, enter 3. Record the built-in trim, not an upgrade or book added later. Unusual combinations are welcome; they can help test the assumptions.
