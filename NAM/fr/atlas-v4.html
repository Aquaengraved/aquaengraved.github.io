Route-planning limits

Map Version 4 is ready for everyday port and market inspection and remains under active refinement. Automatic routes only attempt to avoid land in the current game-derived mask. They do not prove safe water, ship-depth access, travel time, wind boosts or a verified harbour approach. Confirm important journeys in game.

Visual reference: John Pinkerton’s 1818 West Indies chart (public domain). The displayed full-world chart, port positions and automatic routing all remain aligned to the game-derived coastline mask.
