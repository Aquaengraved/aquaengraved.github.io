Captain’s comparison room · public alpha

Ship & Cannon Configurator

Build and compare up to three experimental hull loadouts from current Naval Action API data, or inspect cannons directly. Choose deck guns, construction woods, upgrades and knowledge, then compare transparent raw facts without a universal winner.

Three-configuration workbench

Configure and compare

Configuration A is the reference. Compare B or C with accessible absolute and percentage differences, and show only changed facts when needed. Higher and lower never mean universally better or worse.

Configurator Alpha 0.2

Expanded comparison workbench

Ship-card comparison: speed and the other card values use the ship and selected construction woods. Rating also uses the selected cannons. Books, upgrades, trims, perks and cargo effects are excluded; their source modifiers remain available separately.

Alpha details

Available in this alpha: three ship configurations, accessible absolute and percentage differences, configuration controls, detailed raw hull and logistics facts, and a dedicated three-cannon comparison with deck compatibility evidence.

Source-backed loadouts: choose individual deck guns, frame and planking woods, permanent upgrades and knowledge. Compatible, incompatible and unknown evidence remains distinct; missing values remain unavailable rather than becoming zero.

Quick start

  1. Choose a ship in A, then fit its decks and optional equipment.
  2. Copy A to B or C for a quick variant, or choose another ship.
  3. Review differences below, then compare individual cannons if needed.

Your configuration choices are not uploaded. Use “Save builds in this browser” only if you want these choices restored on this device.
