Working, but unproven

The planner works and produces route candidates, but route validity has not yet been confirmed by independent traders. We need traders to test routes in game and report the results. Treat every route as a test candidate, not a proven recommendation.

Before you trade

This calculator estimates the destination price from the latest market snapshot and an unofficial community formula. The API does not provide a live consuming-port quote or prove that the estimate is the exact price after maintenance. Prices and accepted quantities can fall as players sell there. Profit figures do not include taxes, fees, sailing costs or trading risk. Check both in-game confirmation windows and trade at your own risk.
