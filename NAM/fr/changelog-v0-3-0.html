Les notes de version détaillées sont actuellement disponibles en anglais.

Modifications

  • A modest Feedback link in the header and footer now opens a localized contact page for general feedback, feature requests, bug reports, incorrect data, accessibility concerns, privacy or security concerns, and other questions.
  • The visitor’s email address is required so the developer can request missing information or provide a direct answer; a Discord username remains optional.
  • Bug reports reveal four optional, plain-language prompts. Visitors can answer only what they know, and screenshots can be requested later by email rather than uploaded through the website.
  • The form uses no embedded Formspree widget, CAPTCHA, advertising script, or form cookie. It submits only the visible fields and limited technical context described in the privacy policy.
  • A hidden honeypot provides basic spam protection, while missing or malformed publication configuration disables sending instead of exposing a broken form.
  • The privacy policy now explains Formspree’s necessary request processing and the retention boundaries: up to 30 days at Formspree and no more than 12 months in the separate project mailbox, with earlier deletion when resolved.
  • The complete form was checked in light and dark themes, with keyboard-readable labels and six localized page variants before publication.

Les vues affichées ne sont acceptées qu’à partir d’un instantané agrégé après filtrage des robots connus. Les totaux n’identifient personne, n’utilisent pas de cookies et ne correspondent pas à des visiteurs uniques.
