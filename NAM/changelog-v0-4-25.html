v0.4.25Port actions and careful navigation planning

Map V5 adds direct port actions, Navigator position fixes, and clearly experimental travel-time planning while preserving existing routes and backups.

Date
07-09-2026
Estimated page views
0

What changed

  • Right-click a port or use the visible Port actions button for route start, stop, destination, removal and one-step Undo. The menu also reaches the ledger, suppliers, Navigator, private notes and warehouse planning without changing inventory by itself.
  • Navigator accepts at least three readings to the same ship position from the in-game Trader Tool. A value such as 65k means 65,000 map units. Local screenshot OCR is reviewable, uncertain ports or distances require correction, and manual position correction remains available. The result is an uncertainty area and timestamp, not exact GPS; bearings may follow the next port or a chart bend.
  • Estimate sailing time for your own ships and fleet. Enter each ship's planned cargo; blank speed uses a labelled standard reference. Optional info-panel speed also asks how full the hold was when you read it, so cargo is not counted twice. The slowest ship controls the fleet estimate. Plotted routes include their drawn detours; direct port comparisons remain available. Times are approximate WIP estimates based on community departure and arrival times, with no independent accuracy claim. Example: compare a full outward trip with a half-loaded return before choosing a route. An optional timer compares a frozen prediction with elapsed time for deliberate export. Port notes remain private browser data; the full offline backup includes them by default, while advanced components are optional. Warehouse viewing and pickup planning require explicit profile opt-in. For example, while sailing toward Las Aves, stop the ship, enter three or more Trader Tool port distances, choose Calculate position and then Under way; plotted Route A/B routes remain, and the notes can be restored from the full backup.
