v0.4.20Shipbuilding supplies

See where shipbuilding materials are cheapest, narrow the search to ports you can use, and understand why a price is highlighted.

Date
06-09-2026
Estimated page views
3

What changed

  • For example, to find Iron Ore and Hemp for your next ship, open Shipbuilding supplies from Market and switch to Ports. Look for stops offering several needed materials, then use the map link. The page includes raw resources, construction woods and components from verified ship recipes; items without a priced NPC offer remain visible.
  • Choose all ports, ports within a distance of a starting port, one nation’s ports, one saved profile, or several selected profiles. Prices are compared within that selection. Distance is measured in straight-line map units; it is not a sailing route or travel-cost estimate.
  • Highlighted prices can match or beat a configured game minimum or the lowest price in the displayed history. Check the history coverage and number of recorded days. A recorded low is not a guaranteed game minimum, and port conditions, stock and price modifiers can affect offers.
  • Listed prices exclude port tax and do not include the extra 1 Real used by Recipe cost. Profiles are read only when you select a profile filter; this page does not change warehouse stock.
  • All release notes have been rewritten in clearer player language, with practical examples and explanations of how changes affect existing work where known. The release versions and order are unchanged.
