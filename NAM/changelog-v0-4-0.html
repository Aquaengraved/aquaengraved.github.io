v0.4.0First Ship & Cannon Configurator alpha

Try two ship loadouts side by side in the first, English-language configurator alpha.

Date
30-08-2026
Estimated page views

What changed

  • Wondering what a different battery would weigh? Choose a ship in each configuration, fit compatible cannons to its decks, and compare fitted-gun weight and raw battery damage.
  • You can also explore construction woods, permanent upgrades, and knowledge choices alongside the reported hull facts. Side, bow, stern, and mortar positions remain distinct, and guns must meet all reported deck restrictions.
  • Read the Alpha 0.1 information window before relying on a comparison. Source modifiers are shown separately; unavailable wood modifiers are marked incomplete. The tool does not calculate a verified final in-game result or declare a winner.
  • In this first alpha, configurations are temporary and reset when the page reloads. Saving builds was not part of this release.
